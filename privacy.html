Privacy Policy

Wage Calculator — Everbloom Labs

Last updated: July 2024

Introduction

Welcome to Wage Calculator, developed and maintained by Everbloom Labs. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information We Collect

Wage Calculator does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All data you enter — such as hourly rates, hours worked, or earnings calculations — is processed entirely on your device and is never sent to any external server or third party.

Data Storage

Any settings or data you choose to save within the app are stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage mechanisms. This data remains solely on your device and under your control. Deleting the app will remove all locally stored data.

Third-Party Services

Wage Calculator does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not use crash-reporting SDKs that transmit data to external parties.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we collect no personal data whatsoever, there is no risk of inadvertent collection from any age group.

Your Rights

Because we do not collect or store any personal data, there is no personal information for us to provide, correct, or delete on your behalf. If you have any privacy-related questions or concerns, please contact us at the address below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
